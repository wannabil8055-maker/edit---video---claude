## 1. HOOK (0–3s)
- **0.00–0.25:** Black screen. A single sharp **clock tick** and a **sub-bass hit**. Large white **"8:40"** fades in, centered.
- **0.25–3.00:** Hard cut to the Ustadz in a tight 115% punch-in, then a slow push to 125%. **Audio:** src 8.3–11.0, *"8:40 juga mak dengan ayah terima pahala itu."*
- **On-screen text (top third, 2 lines, appears word by word):**
  - Malay (main): **DOA KAU SAMPAI KE KUBUR MAK AYAH… SAAT ITU JUGA.** ("SAAT ITU JUGA" in gold)
  - English (small, under it): *Your dua reaches your parents' grave… that same second.*
- **Why it works:** a specific number (8:40) plus the grave plus "that same second" makes viewers curious and hits them emotionally inside 3 seconds. The 8:40 line plays again at 7.9s with its full context, so viewers who stay get the payoff.

**Caption style:** Montserrat ExtraBold or Poppins Bold, white with a 4px black stroke and soft shadow, 2–3 words per line, active word in gold (#F2C14E). Place captions at about 62% of screen height, above the TikTok, Reels, and Shorts UI. Malay is the main line and a smaller English line sits underneath.

## 3. PACING & VIBE
- **Music:** a calm, sad nasheed, **vocals or humming only** with no instruments (safer for an Islamic audience). Search CapCut or TikTok sounds for "nasheed humming sad" or "vocal only nasheed". Keep it at **-18 to -22dB** under the voice, use sidechain or auto-ducking, and keep the Ustadz's voice at about -6dB.
- **Rhythm:** a visual change (cut, punch-in, B-roll, or text pop) about **every 2–3s**. Never hold the same frame longer than 4s.
- **Emotional arc:** curiosity (8:40) → reassurance ("no pending") → **peak** ("saat itu juga", music drops) → guilt and reflection ("tak ramai") → call to action.
- **Color grade:** warm, slightly faded (-10 saturation, +warmth, lifted blacks), plus a soft vignette. B-roll a little darker and bluer so the Ustadz feels warm and the grave scenes feel cold.
- **Cleaning the source (important):**
  1. The clip is a **screen recording**: it has the TikTok like and share buttons, old burned-in captions, and another page's watermark. Get the original lecture from Ustadz Wadi Anuar's official channel if you can.
  2. If you can't, crop to remove the right-side UI, put your caption band over the old captions (or blur them), and run CapCut **"Enhance quality"** or a similar upscaler, because the source is only 384x520.
  3. **Credit the source:** "Ustadz Wadi Anuar · source: @originalpage".

## 4. CALL TO ACTION (43–52s)
- **43.0–46.0:** On black, text appears line by line:
  **"Mak ayah kau tengah tunggu."**
  *Your parents are waiting.*
- **46.0–49.5:** **"Berhenti sekejap. Doakan mereka SEKARANG."** (*Pause. Make dua for them NOW.*) with the dua under it:
  **رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ** · *Rabbighfir li wa li walidayya*
- **49.5–52.0:** **"Tulis 'Ameen' 🤲 & share pada seseorang yang dah kehilangan mak/ayah."** (*Comment "Ameen" and share with someone who has lost a parent.*) Small text under it: *HR Muslim 1631: "…atau anak soleh yang mendoakannya."*
- **Loop trick:** end on the clock ticking once and showing "8:40", so the last frame flows straight back into the hook.
