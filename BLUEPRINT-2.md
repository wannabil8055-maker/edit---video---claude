# "HE DIED… AND SMILED." — 2D Islamic Animation Blueprint (Part 2: The Soleh Man)

**Companion to Part 1 "Who Will Answer For You?"** Same world, same faceless art style, **opposite ending**. Post them as a pair ("Part 1 / Part 2") or a duet/stitch.

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 24 fps · **Runtime:** ~60 s · **Language:** English VO + English subtitles
**Voice:** One male narrator. Warm, steady, close-mic, smiling, like a big brother telling you a true story. In the grave scene he goes quiet and full of awe, then bursts into joy.

---

## 0. RULES FOR RESPECTFUL CONTENT

| Topic | How to handle it |
|---|---|
| Angels of mercy | **No faces.** Tall shapes of soft white-gold light with wings drawn as light streaks. The hadith says their faces are bright "like the sun", so show that as a pure glow, not a face. |
| Munkar & Nakir | Same design as Part 1 (deep blue and black smoke-light with lightning eyes). That keeps the series consistent. They are still awe-inspiring here, but the scene turns to **light** once he answers. |
| Quran recitation & selawat | **Never use AI or TTS to recite the Quran or sing selawat.** Use a licensed recording of a real qari or munshid, or leave it as soft ambience and show the Arabic on screen. |
| Hidden sadaqah | Show it **without any showing off**: at night, a face hidden under a hood, money slipped under a door. He never takes a selfie. |
| Jannah | Show it **only as a glimpse through a door**: light, rivers, gardens. The point is that it is beyond imagination, so we never show it fully. |
| Sources (put in caption) | The believer's answers, "My servant has spoken the truth", the widened grave and the door to Jannah, and the angels with faces like the sun: *Abu Dawud 4753 / Musnad Ahmad (hadith of al-Bara' ibn 'Azib)*. Hidden charity: *Bukhari 1423*. Deeds that continue after death: *Muslim 1631*. *"O reassured soul…"*: *Al-Fajr 89:27-30*. Have an ustaz check the script before posting. |

## 1. VISUAL STYLE & AESTHETIC

**Style name: "Paper-Light Dawn"**. It uses the same flat 2D cut-paper look as Part 1 (faceless characters, thick rounded outlines, paper-grain texture, layered parallax), but **lit by dawn instead of neon**.

- **Signature device: the golden deed thread (callback to Part 1).** In Part 1 it frayed and snapped. Here it **gets thicker with every good deed**. In the grave it **becomes a rope of light** that pulls open the door to Jannah. At the end, threads from his children keep arriving, so it **never stops growing**.
- **Motion language:**
  - *Dunya scenes*: calm, confident, smooth easing, gentle bounce. It feels like a happy, productive life, not a hollow one.
  - *Sickness & death*: slow and soft, but **not frightening**. Light gets brighter as things slow down.
  - *Grave Q&A*: dark and tense at first, then each answer **cracks the dark with gold light**.
  - *Barzakh garden*: floaty, lush, with particles drifting upward. Everything breathes.

### Colour palette

| Act | Colours | Hex | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dunya (barakah)** | Dawn gold, sage green, soft teal, ivory, on warm cream | `#F2C14E` `#7BAE7F` `#5EC4B6` `#FFF8EC` `#FBEBD3` | Peace, purpose, warmth |
| **Death & grave (before answers)** | Ink black, bruise blue, bone white | `#05060A` `#1E3A8A` `#E8E4D9` | Awe, seriousness |
| **Barzakh garden (after answers)** | Emerald, river turquoise, pearl white, honey gold | `#10B981` `#2DD4BF` `#F8FAFC` `#FBBF24` | Relief, reward, joy |

**Typeface:** Montserrat ExtraBold captions (white with a soft dark stroke, key word in **gold**). Arabic in Amiri.

## 2. THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:03)

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.6 s | Pure black. A white heart-monitor line beats twice… then **goes flat** (same shot as Part 1). | — | *beep… beep… beeeeep* |
| 0.6–1.6 s | The flat line **turns gold**, lifts off the screen and curls into a **smile shape**, then blooms into a glowing doorway. | **"HE DIED…"** slams in (white, screen shake). Then **"…AND SMILED."** pops in gold with a light burst. | Deep boom, then a **bright shimmer** and a soft "ahh" choir swell (voice only). |
| 1.6–3.0 s | The camera **flies through the doorway of light** and comes out at the window of a quiet house at Fajr, then pushes in on the man reading the Quran. | Small top line: **"Watch till the end. This could be YOU."** | Light whoosh, birds, and a faint adhan of Fajr (clear, not muffled, the reverse of Part 1). VO: *"He died… and he smiled. Want to know why?"* |

> Why it works: people who watched Part 1 recognise the flatline straight away and expect horror. The twist to gold is the pattern break. "He died and smiled" creates a curiosity gap.

## 3. SCENE-BY-SCENE ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

### ACT 1 — A LIFE OF BARAKAH (0:03 – 0:24)

**SCENE 1 — Fajr & Quran**
- **VO:** *"His mornings start with the Quran."*
- **Visual:** The same bedroom layout as Part 1, but tidy, with the window glowing at dawn. The prayer mat is **unrolled**. He sits on it with a mushaf on a rehal. Gold letters float up off the page like fireflies and **feed into his golden thread**, which grows thicker. His phone sits face-down and charging.
- **Text:** Caption word by word. A small Arabic word **اقْرَأْ** floats up and dissolves.
- **SFX:** Birds, a soft page turn, a gentle breath. *(Optional: a licensed soft Quran recitation at low volume.)*

**SCENE 2 — Orphans' Community**
- **VO:** *"His weekends? With the orphans."*
- **Visual:** A community hall with paper bunting. He sits on the floor with a group of kids (faceless, all different heights). He hands out school bags and laughs. A kid jumps on his back. Small hearts rise and **turn into gold threads**.
- **Text:** **"Weekends = Orphans"** pops in like a sticker label.
- **SFX:** Children's laughter, paper rustle, a soft "yay!", playful pizzicato pops.

**SCENE 3 — Secret Charity**
- **VO:** *"He gives in secret. His left hand never knows what his right hand gave."*
- **Visual:** Night, a quiet street. He's in a hood with his face hidden and slips an envelope under a poor family's door, then walks away fast. **Split zoom:** his right hand gives, while his left hand is behind his back, "not watching". The door opens later and a mother's silhouette holds the envelope to her chest.
- **Text:** **"Right hand gives. Left hand never knows."**, with "never knows" fading to a whisper.
- **SFX:** Crickets, soft footsteps, envelope slide *shhk*, a door creaks open, a soft gasp of relief, a gentle chime.

**SCENE 4 — The Riches DROP… and he gives them away**
- **VO:** *"Bugatti. Rolls-Royce. A villa on the hill… He had it all. But he wanted the hereafter more."*
- **Visual:** The **same gedebuk drops as Part 1**, but in daylight. The hypercar, then the luxury car, then the villa each **SLAM** down with dust and a crack in the ground. He doesn't pose. He calmly **hands the keys to an orphanage van** and to a family. The villa turns into a **"Rumah Anak Yatim / Orphan Home"** sign with kids running in. A counter in the corner **goes DOWN** ("Wealth: −$") while a gold **"Akhirah: +∞"** counter goes up.
- **Text:** Item names drop in ("BUGATTI." "ROLLS-ROYCE." "VILLA.") and then get **stamped "GIVEN."** in green.
- **SFX:** Whistle-fall → **heavy thud ×3**, keys jingle, kids cheering, a warm "Alhamdulillah" murmur, a cash register **running in reverse**.

**SCENE 5 — Debt-free, Tahajjud, Dhuha, Masjid**
- **VO:** *"Every debt paid. Tahajjud at night. Dhuha in the morning. The masjid… his second home."*
- **Visual:** A fast, satisfying montage of about 1 s each:
  1. A notebook of IOUs where each line gets a **✔ PAID** stamp and the book closes.
  2. **3 a.m.**: a moonlit room, him alone in sujud, with stars outside.
  3. **Sunrise**: Dhuha by an open window, sunlight across the mat.
  4. **Masjid**: he walks in, first row, shoes lined up neatly, and a footstep counter turns into gold coins.
- **Text:** A kinetic checklist ticks itself off: **"✔ Debt-free ✔ Tahajjud ✔ Dhuha ✔ Masjid"**.
- **SFX:** Stamp thunk ×3, a night owl, a soft wind chime, morning birds, masjid ambience (shuffling feet, a soft "Allahu Akbar" from an imam in the distance), a short selawat hum *(licensed recording)*.

### ACT 2 — A BEAUTIFUL ENDING (0:24 – 0:34)

**SCENE 6 — Sickness**
- **VO:** *"Then one day… he fell sick."*
- **Visual:** A hospital bed by a window. A drip and a monitor. His family is around him, and his son reads Yasin from a phone. He lifts his index finger (**shahadah**). The heart line slows… beep… flat. **Unlike Part 1, the room gets brighter, not darker.**
- **Text:** Under his raised finger, small Arabic: **لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰه**.
- **SFX:** Slow monitor beeps, soft rain on the window, a whispered family "Ameen", then the flatline, which **fades into a gentle chime instead of a scream**.

**SCENE 7 — Angels of Mercy**
- **VO:** *"Angels of mercy came… faces bright like the sun… racing to receive his soul. No fear. Only relief."*
- **Visual:** The ceiling dissolves into soft light. **Many tall angels of white-gold light** descend in rows, carrying a shroud of light and a scent shown as swirling golden mist. His soul rises **gently, smiling** (the body language is relaxed shoulders and an open chest), "like a drop of water from a jug". The angels surround him and the camera rises with them.
- **Text:** The verse fades in, glowing: **يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ**, with *"O reassured soul… return to your Lord, well-pleased."* (Al-Fajr 89:27-28) underneath.
- **SFX:** A rising choir of voices only, soft wings fluttering, one relieved exhale **"ahh…"**, and bright shimmer particles.

**SCENE 8 — Washed, Wrapped, Carried, Buried**
- **VO:** *"They washed him. Wrapped him in white. Carried him… and laid him in the ground."*
- **Visual:** The same four shots as Part 1, but **warm**: a gentle glow on the body, **a big crowd** at the janazah filling the frame (the orphans are there too, with the kids holding hands), and when the grave closes, **a faint gold glow stays in the soil**.
- **Text:** **"Washed." → "Wrapped." → "Carried." → "Laid to rest."**
- **SFX:** Water pouring, cloth folding, many footsteps and a crowd murmuring "La ilaha illallah", soil thuds ×4 (soft, not harsh).

### ACT 3 — THE GRAVE (0:34 – 0:52)

**SCENE 9 — Munkar & Nakir**
- **VO:** *"Then two angels came. Munkar… and Nakir."*
- **Visual:** The dark grave, exactly like Part 1. Two giant blue-black smoke forms rise and lightning eyes open. **But his kafan glows softly gold**, and he is **sitting up calmly**, not curled up in fear.
- **SFX:** Earth rumble, thunder, a low choir. Then silence.

**SCENE 10 — The Three Questions, The Three Answers**
- **VO:**
  - Angels: **"Man rabbuka?"** — Him (calm, clear): *"My Lord is Allah."*
  - Angels: **"Ma dinuka?"** — *"My religion is Islam."*
  - Angels: **"Man nabiyyuka?"** — *"My prophet is Muhammad, peace be upon him."*
- **Visual:** Each question sends a blue shockwave. **Each answer sends a golden shockwave back** that **pushes the darkness away**, so the frame gets brighter step by step. His good deeds from Act 1 appear as **glowing shields** around him: the Quran, the orphans, the envelope, the sujud.
- **Text:** Arabic question (Amiri, blue glow), then his answer slams in big in **gold**: **"ALLAH." "ISLAM." "MUHAMMAD ﷺ."**
- **SFX:** Question = deep boom. Answer = **bright bell + warm swell + sparkle**. Each one more joyful than the last.

**SCENE 11 — The Grave Opens Up**
- **VO:** *"A voice from the heavens: My servant has spoken the truth. His grave was widened… as far as the eye can see. A door opened… to his place in Jannah."*
- **Visual:** The soil walls **slide away** like stage curtains. The dark turns into an endless glowing meadow. A **golden door** appears, the golden thread pulls it open, and light floods out. He stands up in white, looking out at gardens, rivers and pavilions in the far distance.
- **Text:** **"My servant has spoken the truth."** appears in elegant serif, softly glowing. Then **"As far as the eye can see."**
- **SFX:** A huge warm swell, a door unlocking (a deep, soft *clunk*), a rushing river, birdsong, wind through leaves.

**SCENE 12 — Not Redang Island. A Garden of Paradise.**
- **VO:** *"Not a holiday island… a garden of Paradise."*
- **Visual:** A **fake travel ad** pops up for a postcard tropical island with a "✈ BOOK NOW" button. It **gets swiped away** to reveal the real garden: rivers of light, trees with gold leaves, lanterns floating. (A small joke for the youth audience, then the real reward.)
- **Text:** **"Holiday Island ❌"** slides off. Then **"Garden of Jannah ✔"** blooms in.
- **SFX:** A cheesy ad jingle that **record-scratches** off, then a lush ambience.

**SCENE 13 — Rewards Keep Arriving**
- **VO:** *"And rewards keep arriving… from the children he raised. Every du'a. Every good deed. Non-stop."*
- **Visual:** **Split screen (a mirror of Part 1).** Top: his grown son and daughter make du'a after solat and give food to orphans. A loading bar **"Sending du'a…" fills to 100% "Delivered ✔"**. Bottom: in his garden, **gold parcels of light** fly down and land around him. A counter reads **"Rewards received: +1 +7 +99 … ∞"**.
- **Text:** **"Delivered ✔"** and the counter rolling up.
- **SFX:** Notification dings that sound **like soft bells**, a parcel whoosh, kids saying "Ameen".

**SCENE 14 — Busy in Barzakh**
- **VO:** *"Now, his life in Barzakh is very busy… busy enjoying."*
- **Visual:** He reclines under a tree by the river. A sand timer floats, and the falling sand is **golden light**. A **"BUSY" stamp lands, in GREEN this time** (callback to Part 1's red stamp), and then a small **"enjoying 😊"** label.
- **SFX:** Stamp *thunk* (same sound as Part 1), a happy laugh, river sounds.

### ACT 4 — CTA (0:52 – 0:60)

- **VO:** *"Will your death look like this? Start today. Comment Ameen… and share this."*
- **Visual:** The golden thread from the garden **reaches out of the screen toward the viewer** and forms a phone outline (callback to Part 1's 3% battery). This time **the battery charges up to 100%** in green, with a gentle beat. The threads split into hundreds of lights flying out (sharing).
- **Text:**
  - Top: **"Part 1: Who will answer for you?"** (small, tappable-looking chip)
  - Centre (big, gold, pulsing): **"WILL YOUR DEATH LOOK LIKE THIS?"**
  - Bottom: **"Comment: AMEEN 🤲"** · **"Share = sadaqah jariyah 🤍"**
- **Loop:** It fades to the gold smile-line from the hook, so the replay loops seamlessly.
- **SFX:** A steady, healthy heartbeat, a charging chime, a final warm chord.
