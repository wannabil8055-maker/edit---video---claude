**Series:** Part 1 *Who Will Answer For You?* · Part 2 *He Died… and Smiled* · Part 3 *Every Grave Will Open* · **Part 4 *Allah Chose You***

## 1. VISUAL STYLE & AESTHETIC

**Style name: "Paper-Lantern Night"**. It uses the same flat 2D cut-paper style as the series (faceless figures, rounded outlines, paper grain), set in a **deep, calm night sky**.

- **Signature device: Du'a lanterns.** Every du'a leaves the hands as a **glowing paper lantern** with the words written inside it, and it floats up through the layers of sky. The series' **golden thread** ties each lantern to the hands that sent it, so it is never lost.
- **Motion language:** slow, floating, breathing. Lanterns rise with gentle sway, stars twinkle, and light pours like liquid into cupped hands. Energy kicks in only at "Ask BIG", where small wishes shrink and a huge golden lantern rises.

Typeface: Montserrat ExtraBold captions with gold key words. Arabic in Amiri.

## 3. SCENE-BY-SCENE ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

**SCENE 1 — One Window Among Billions (0:03 – 0:09)**
- **VO:** *"Billions of people. Millions of hearts asleep. But tonight… your hands went up."*
- **Visual:** A night city seen from above, with thousands of tiny window lights blinking out one by one as people sleep. **One window glows gold.** Zoom in to a young man on a prayer mat with his hands raised. A golden lantern lifts from his palms.
- **Text:** Counter in the corner: **"8,000,000,000 people"**, then **"1 heart: YOU"** in gold.
- **SFX:** City hum fading out, clock ticking, a soft sniff and whisper ("Ya Allah…"), and a lantern *whoomp* as it lifts.

**SCENE 2 — The Answer Comes With It (0:09 – 0:18)**
- **VO:** *"Umar ibn al-Khattab once said: I don't worry about the answer. I worry about making du'a. Because when du'a comes… the answer comes with it."*
- **Visual:** A parchment quote card unrolls. Then a visual metaphor: the du'a is shown as a **golden key**. The key flies into a giant sky door, which turns and unlocks, and light spills out.
- **Text:** The quote in elegant serif, word by word. Then **"The du'a IS the sign."** pulses in gold. A small "— Umar ibn al-Khattab (attributed)" sits under the quote.
- **SFX:** Parchment rustle, a key turning (deep *clack-clunk*), door creak, a choir-hum swell.

**SCENE 3 — Never Empty (0:18 – 0:24)**
- **VO:** *"Your Lord is too generous… to return your raised hands empty."*
- **Visual:** A close-up of cupped hands. Golden light **pours down like honey** and fills them until it overflows, then drips into flowers growing below.
- **Text:** **"Never empty."** with a soft pulse. Small "(Abu Dawud 1488)".
- **SFX:** Liquid-light pour, a gentle chime cascade, a relieved exhale.

**SCENE 4 — Ask BIG (0:24 – 0:31)**
- **VO:** *"So ask. Ask BIG. Not just exams… or money… or a car."*
- **Visual:** Small floating icons (📚 exam, 💰 money, 🚗 car, 📱 phone) circle the man. Each one **shrinks with a comic "pop"** as the VO names it. Then he takes a breath and **lifts a HUGE lantern** that fills the whole frame.
- **Text:** **"ASK BIG."** slams in, then scales up and fills the screen.
- **SFX:** Three small pops, a playful pluck, then a big bass *boom* and the hum rises.

**SCENE 5 — Jannatul Firdaus (0:31 – 0:40)**
- **VO:** *"Ask for Jannatul Firdaus. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: when you ask Allah, ask for Firdaus. The highest level of Paradise."*
- **Visual:** The giant lantern rises through **layered skies**: a level meter climbs **1… 10… 50… 100**. It reaches the top: a radiant emerald-and-gold garden glowing above the clouds. Arabic **الفردوس الأعلى** shimmers above it.
- **Text:** Level meter **"Level 100: AL-FIRDAUS"** in gold. Small "(Bukhari 2790)".
- **SFX:** An ascending whoosh, a rising arpeggio of chimes for each level, birds and river at the top, a majestic hum chord.

**SCENE 6 — Every Du'a Is Answered (0:40 – 0:48)**
- **VO:** *"And every du'a is answered. He gives it now… saves it for your hereafter… or protects you from a harm you never saw."*
- **Visual:** Three glowing cards flip in one by one:
  1. 🎁 **A gift box opens**: "Given now".
  2. 🏆 **A treasure chest locks in the clouds**: "Saved for Akhirah".
  3. 🛡️ **A shield of light deflects a falling rock** over a sleeping figure: "Harm removed".
- **Text:** Each card label pops in with a check mark. Small "(Ahmad 11133)".
- **SFX:** Gift ribbon *zip* and pop, chest *clunk* and sparkle, rock whoosh and shield *clang*.

**SCENE 7 — The Golden Moments (0:48 – 0:52)**
- **VO:** *"So tonight… in sujud, before Fajr, raise your hands."*
- **Visual:** Fast pills appear around a man in **sujud** under a starry window: **"🌙 Last third of the night" · "🧎 In sujud" · "🌧️ When it rains" · "🕌 Between adhan & iqamah"**. Rain falls on the window, and lanterns rise from the city, now lots of them.
- **SFX:** Soft rain, a distant owl, a gentle heartbeat, the hum.

## 4. CALL TO ACTION (CTA) ANIMATION (0:52 – 0:57)

- **VO:** *"Type one du'a in the comments. Someone will say Ameen for you."*
- **Visual:** A phone **comment box** slides up and types: *"Ya Allah, grant me Jannatul Firdaus 🤲"*. Then **"Ameen" hearts** fly up from everywhere, and each turns into a little lantern rising.
- **Text:** **"Comment your du'a 🤲"** (big, gold) · **"Reply Ameen to others"** · **"Share = sadaqah jariyah 🤍"**
- **Loop:** The lanterns rise to black, and the cupped hands from the hook rise again.
- **SFX:** Keyboard taps, a send *whoosh*, a flurry of soft "ding" hearts, final hum chord.
