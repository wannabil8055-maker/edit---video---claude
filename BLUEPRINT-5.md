# "MODEST IS THE NEW STYLISH." — 2D Islamic Animation Blueprint (Part 5: Aurah & Style)

**Series:** Part 1–4 · **Part 5 *Modest Is the New Stylish***
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 24 fps · **Runtime:** ~55 s · **Language:** English VO + English subtitles
**Voice:** One male narrator. Upbeat and supportive, like a big brother hyping up his sister. **Never preachy and never shaming.** He drops to soft and sincere for the ayah and hadith, then becomes playful again for the outfit montage.

---

## 0. ACCURACY & RESPECT NOTES

| Point | How we handle it |
|---|---|
| The command | *"…tell the believing women… to draw their head-coverings over their chests…"* (An-Nur 24:31) and *"…draw their outer garments over themselves…"* (Al-Ahzab 33:59). |
| Aurah for women | Majority view: the whole body except the face and hands, in front of non-mahram men. |
| Clothing conditions | Covers the aurah; **loose, not tight**; **thick, not see-through**; not overly flashy or attention-seeking (*tabarruj*). These are shown as a friendly checklist, not a lecture. |
| Beauty is allowed | *"Indeed Allah is beautiful and He loves beauty."* (Sahih Muslim 91). This is the green light for colour, pattern and elegance *within* the guidelines. |
| Visual rules | **No uncovered "before" shot and no body-contour drawings.** "Tight vs. loose" and "see-through vs. thick" are shown with **fabric swatches and icons**, never bodies. The main character is a faceless, covered woman from start to finish. **No shaming of other women.** |
| Responsibility callback | Part 1 showed the husband's responsibility to guide. Here we add one supportive line for brothers, fathers and husbands: support and guide with kindness. |
| Review | Have an ustaz or ustazah check the script before posting. |

## 1. VISUAL STYLE & AESTHETIC

**Style name: "Paper-Cut Runway"**. It uses the same flat 2D cut-paper style as the series (faceless figures, rounded shapes, paper grain), styled like a **fashion magazine**: big bold type, spotlight runway, colour swatches, sticker labels, and "OOTD" (outfit of the day) frames.

- **Main character:** "Aisyah", a faceless young woman in hijab. Outfits are built as layered paper shapes: a draped tudung covering the hair, neck and chest, plus a long loose outfit.
- **Signature device: the golden thread** (series callback). It becomes a **golden sewing thread** that stitches each new outfit together. Every outfit is "stitched with taqwa".
- **Motion language:** snappy fashion-reel cuts on the beat, outfit swaps with a **spin + sparkle wipe**, fabric that sways softly, sticker labels popping on with a bounce.

### Colour palette

| Use | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Backgrounds | Blush cream, warm ivory | `#FDF2EC` `#FFF8EC` |
| Outfits | Dusty rose, sage, lilac, emerald, mocha, navy | `#E8A0A8` `#9CB59A` `#C4B5FD` `#10B981` `#8B6B4A` `#1E3A8A` |
| Accents | Soft gold (thread, crowns, sparkle) | `#E9B949` |
| "Boring" opening | Flat greys | `#9CA3AF` `#6B7280` |

Typeface: Montserrat ExtraBold for magazine headlines. Amiri for Arabic.

## 3. SCENE-BY-SCENE ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

**SCENE 1 — "Hijab = Boring?" (0:03 – 0:09)**
- **VO:** *"Meet Aisyah. She loves fashion. But she was scared covering up meant… boring."*
- **Visual:** Her room. A wardrobe full of **grey, shapeless** clothes. She stands in a flat grey outfit, shoulders slumped. A thought bubble shows a sad grey ghost-shaped sack with "boring?".
- **Text:** **"Hijab = boring?"** in a grey, deflating font. The question mark droops.
- **SFX:** A sad trombone-style "wah" (a voice hum), a hanger clack, a sigh.

**SCENE 2 — She Loves Allah More (0:09 – 0:16)**
- **VO:** *"But she loves Allah more. And Allah tells the believing women to draw their coverings over themselves."*
- **Visual:** She opens the Quran by the window at sunset. Gold Arabic floats up (**وَلْيَضْرِبْنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّ**). The golden thread rises from the page and **wraps around her like a measuring tape**, ready to stitch.
- **Text:** Translation fades in: *"…draw their coverings over their chests…" (24:31)*.
- **SFX:** Page turn, a soft hum swell, a gentle chime.

**SCENE 3 — Style WITHIN Deen (0:16 – 0:21)**
- **VO:** *"So it's not style OR deen. It's style… within deen."*
- **Visual:** Two big words, **STYLE** and **DEEN**, separated by **"OR"**. The "OR" gets **cut with gold scissors** and replaced by **"WITHIN"**. A circle labelled DEEN wraps around STYLE.
- **Text:** **"STYLE ✂ OR → WITHIN DEEN"**.
- **SFX:** Scissor *snip*, sticker *pop*, a satisfying click.

**SCENE 4 — The 4-Point Checklist (0:21 – 0:31)**
- **VO:** *"The checklist: Covers all, except face and hands. Loose, not tight. Thick, not see-through. Elegant, not attention-seeking."*
- **Visual:** A clipboard with a **pastel checklist**. Each line pops in with a **fabric-swatch demo** (no bodies):
  1. 🧕 A covered silhouette icon with face and hands highlighted ✔
  2. A **tight band vs. a flowy drape** of fabric → flowy ✔
  3. **Sheer mesh swatch vs. thick fabric swatch** with light shining through → thick ✔
  4. **Flashy neon-glitter vs. elegant soft tone** → elegant ✔
- **Text:** Each item ticks off with a gold ✔.
- **SFX:** Pen tick ×4, fabric swish, a soft "ding".

**SCENE 5 — Then… GO STYLISH (0:31 – 0:42) — the glow-up montage**
- **VO:** *"And then? Go stylish. A flowy emerald abaya. Pastel tudung with a long cardigan. Wide pants and a long tunic. Even sporty, for the gym."*
- **Visual:** The colours **flood back**. Beat-synced **outfit spins** (≈2 s each). The golden thread stitches each look on with a sparkle wipe:
  1. **Emerald abaya** with gold trim and a soft cream tudung.
  2. **Dusty rose tudung + long sage cardigan + flowy skirt.**
  3. **Long lilac tunic + wide navy pants**, with a tote bag.
  4. **Sporty**: a sports hijab, loose long top, joggers and sneakers.
- **Text:** Magazine **OOTD frame** labels pop on each look: **"OOTD #1 Elegant" · "#2 Soft Girl" · "#3 Campus" · "#4 Sporty"**.
- **SFX:** Whoosh-spin, a sparkle chime per outfit, camera shutters, fabric swish.

**SCENE 6 — Allah Loves Beauty (0:42 – 0:47)**
- **VO:** *"The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Allah is beautiful, and He loves beauty."*
- **Visual:** Soft focus. Aisyah stands in golden light in her emerald look. Flowers bloom around her feet and the golden thread forms a halo-like ring above her (not a halo, a gold **crown outline**).
- **Text:** *"Allah is beautiful and loves beauty." (Muslim 91)* fades in, serif, gold.
- **SFX:** Gentle chime cascade, soft hum chord.

**SCENE 7 — Not a Cage. A Crown. (0:47 – 0:52)**
- **VO:** *"Her hijab isn't a cage. It's a crown. And brothers… support her."*
- **Visual:** A **birdcage outline** flies at her, **shatters into gold sparkles**, and re-forms as a **gold crown** above her head. A quick cutaway shows her father and brother (faceless) giving a thumbs-up, and her father handing her a gift box with a new tudung.
- **Text:** **"NOT A CAGE ✖"** → **"A CROWN 👑"** (the cage text breaks, the crown text glows).
- **SFX:** Glass-sparkle shatter, crown *shing*, a warm "aww" hum.

## 4. CALL TO ACTION (CTA) ANIMATION (0:52 – 0:57)

- **VO:** *"Sisters, drop your favourite modest outfit in the comments. And share this with a friend."*
- **Visual:** The four outfits line up on a **runway**, then **freeze in a magazine-cover frame** titled **"MODEST IS THE NEW STYLISH"**. A comment bubble pops: *"Emerald abaya 😍"*. Hearts float.
- **Text:** **"Your fav modest OOTD? 👇"** · **"Tag a sister who slays in hijab 🤍"** · **"Share = sadaqah jariyah"**
- **Loop:** The spotlight clicks off to black, which returns to the hook's spotlight slam.
- **SFX:** Runway beat outro, camera flashes, pop of the comment bubble, spotlight *clunk*.
