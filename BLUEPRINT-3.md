# "EVERY GRAVE WILL OPEN." — 2D Islamic Animation Blueprint (Part 3: Mahsyar)

**Series:** Part 1 *Who Will Answer For You?* → Part 2 *He Died… and Smiled* → **Part 3 *Every Grave Will Open***
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 24 fps · **Runtime:** ~60 s · **Language:** English VO + English subtitles
**Voice:** One male narrator. Deep and cinematic. Big, slow and full of awe for the Mahsyar scenes; tense and quick on the Sirat; warm and glowing at Jannah; heavy and quiet at the fall. One second male voice (shaky) plays the man who gets his book in the left hand.

| Topic | How to handle it |

## 1. VISUAL STYLE & AESTHETIC

**Style name: "Paper-Light Epic"**. It uses the same flat 2D cut-paper style as Parts 1–2, but on a **huge scale**. The camera is pulled far back, with crowds of thousands of tiny silhouettes and a huge sky. It cuts between **wide shots that feel tiny and overwhelming** and **close-ups of one man and his golden thread**.

- **Signature device: the golden thread becomes a LIGHT.** On the Sirat, everyone's deeds glow as a light at their feet. A strong thread means a blazing torch. A frayed thread means a flicker that keeps going out.
- **Motion language:** crowds move like a slow ocean. Books fly like a flock of birds. The Scale moves heavily, with a big settle-and-bounce. Sirat crossings are speed lines: streaks for lightning, swirls for wind, wing flaps for birds, and slow, shaky drags for the crawlers. Jannah is soft, floaty and fully lit.

## 3. SCENE-BY-SCENE ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

### ACT 1 — THE GATHERING (0:03 – 0:12)

**SCENE 1 — Resurrection & the Plain of Mahsyar**
- **VO:** *"Every human who ever lived… rises. Barefoot. Waiting. Under a burning sun."*
- **Visual:** A flat, endless plain. The sun hangs **very low and huge** with heat shimmer. An ocean of silhouettes stands shoulder to shoulder. Close-up on feet in hot sand, with sweat drops (shown as stylised drops) rising around people. Some shield their eyes. Everyone looks up.
- **Text:** **"Day of Judgement"** fades in at the top with a heat-haze wobble.
- **SFX:** Low wind, a vast murmuring crowd, heat sizzle, a **slow, heavy heartbeat**.

### ACT 2 — THE BOOKS (0:12 – 0:21)

**SCENE 2 — The Books Fly**
- **VO:** *"Then the books fly… your whole life, written."*
- **Visual:** The sky fills with **thousands of books** flapping like birds. They swirl, then dive toward people, each locked onto its owner. Pages flicker past showing tiny moments: the phone, the prayer mat, the envelope.
- **SFX:** A rush of **pages fluttering** like a huge flock, whooshes passing the camera, then a hard silence just before they land.

**SCENE 3 — Right Hand vs. Left Hand (split screen)**
- **VO:** *"Some receive it in the right hand… and smile. Others, in the left…"* — Left-hand man (shaky): *"I wish I was never given my book!"*
- **Visual:** **Top:** the golden-thread man catches his book in his **right hand**. It glows gold and he lifts it high to show others: *"Here, read my book!"* (69:19). **Bottom:** a grey figure, with the book arriving in his **left hand** from behind his back. It's dark, smoking, and he falls to his knees.
- **Text:** Top in gold: **"RIGHT HAND ✔"**. Bottom in red: **"LEFT HAND ✖"**.
- **SFX:** Top: a bright chime and a happy exhale. Bottom: a dull thud, a sob, and a scared heartbeat speeding up.

### ACT 3 — THE SCALE (0:21 – 0:29)

**SCENE 4 — The Mizan**
- **VO:** *"Then the Scale. Every atom of good. Every atom of evil. Heavy good… joy. Heavy evil… ruin."*
- **Visual:** A colossal scale of light fills the vertical frame, with pans the size of mountains. Gold deed-cubes (prayer, charity, Quran) drop into the right pan, and dark cubes into the left. **Two outcomes, split:**
  1. The pan sinks with good → gold light bursts, and confetti made of light rises.
  2. The pan sinks with evil → the chains strain and crack, and the light goes red.
- **Text:** **"⚖ HEAVY GOOD = JOY"** in gold, then **"⚖ HEAVY EVIL = RUIN"** in red. Each pulses with the settle.
- **SFX:** Heavy **metal chains creaking**, deep *clunk* as each cube lands, a long suspense drone, a **heartbeat that stops** while the scale wobbles, then a bright swell (good) or a low brass hit (evil).

### ACT 4 — THE HAWDH (0:29 – 0:34)

**SCENE 5 — The Pool**
- **VO:** *"The believers reach the Pool… one sip, and never thirsty again."*
- **Visual:** After the heat, a sudden **cool, glowing pool**, whiter than milk with gold light rippling across it. Cups of light rest at the edge, "as many as the stars". The golden-thread man takes a cup and drinks, his shoulders drop in relief, and a cool blue wave washes over the frame and cools the heat haze.
- **Text:** **"Never thirsty again."** in soft aqua, with a water-ripple distortion.
- **SFX:** Water pouring, a **gulp**, a long relieved **"ahhh…"** (the crowd murmur drops away), a gentle crystal chime and soft water ambience.

### ACT 5 — THE SIRAT (0:34 – 0:48)

**SCENE 6 — The Bridge**
- **VO:** *"Then the Bridge. Sirat. Thinner than hair, sharper than a sword… over Hell."*
- **Visual:** A **hair-thin glowing line** stretches over a black void with a **deep red glow churning far below**. Hooks hang along its sides. A camera tilt-down shows the drop and gives a feeling of vertigo.
- **SFX:** Fire roaring deep below, wind howling, a high, thin metallic ring (the "blade"), and a heartbeat.

**SCENE 7 — The Speeds (rapid-fire montage, about 0.7 s each)**
- **VO:** *"Some cross in a blink. Like lightning. Like wind. Like birds. Some run. Some walk. Some… crawl."*
- **Visual:** The same bridge, with a **different crosser each beat**:
  1. **Blink:** a figure is there and then instantly on the other side (a frame cut and a flash).
  2. **Lightning:** a zig-zag streak of light across.
  3. **Wind:** a swirling blur with leaf-like particles.
  4. **Birds:** a figure gliding with wing-shaped light streaks.
  5. **Running:** fast legs and a dust trail.
  6. **Walking:** slow and careful, with arms out for balance.
  7. **Crawling:** inching along on hands and knees, slipping and gripping, while the red glow reaches up.
- **Text:** Each speed word **slams in** in a matching style: **"BLINK."** (flash) · **"LIGHTNING."** (jagged) · **"WIND."** (blur) · **"BIRDS."** (floaty) · **"RUN." "WALK."** · **"crawl…"** (small, shaky, dragging letters).
- **SFX:** Blink = a tiny *tick*. Lightning = a thunder crack. Wind = a whoosh. Birds = wing flaps. Run = fast footsteps. Walk = slow steps. Crawl = **cloth dragging, heavy breathing, a whimper, fire flaring up**.

**SCENE 8 — Their Lights**
- **VO:** *"Each one carries a light… as bright as their deeds. For some… it barely flickers."*
- **Visual:** Darkness. The bridge is visible only where there's light. The golden-thread man holds a **blazing torch** that lights the bridge far ahead. Behind him, someone's light is a **flickering spark at his toe** that **goes out**, and he freezes in the dark, unable to see his next step.
- **Text:** **"Your deeds = your light."** It glows and then flickers out at the end.
- **SFX:** Torch whoosh, a flicker crackle, and then a **click into silence** when the light goes out.

### ACT 6 — JANNAH (0:48 – 0:56)

**SCENE 9 — The Gates & Your Palace**
- **VO:** *"Those who cross… enter Paradise through its gates. They know their palace… better than their home on earth."*
- **Visual:** Huge golden gates swing open (8 gates, with names shown faintly, like **Ar-Rayyan**). Light floods out. The man walks in **without needing directions**, turns left, then right, and smiles at **his** palace. A small callback shows him walking home in the dunya, but now it's **his palace**.
- **Text:** **"You'll know your way home."**
- **SFX:** A massive warm swell, gates opening (deep, soft rumble), choir pads, birds, a river, a happy laugh.

**SCENE 10 — Forever**
- **VO:** *"No pain. No worry. No sleep needed. Forever… busy enjoying."*
- **Visual:** Quick icons **crossed out** one by one: 🤕 pain → ✖, 😟 worry → ✖, 😴 sleep → ✖. He relaxes in the garden with family by the river, and the **"BUSY" green stamp** lands (series callback).
- **SFX:** Three soft "pop-cross" sounds, then the stamp *thunk* and a joyful chord.

### ACT 7 — THE OTHER ENDING (0:56 – 0:58)

**SCENE 11 — Those Who Fail**
- **VO:** *"And those who fail… fall."*
- **Visual:** **Smash cut** from the bright Jannah to the black Sirat. A silhouette loses grip and **falls away** into the red glow, getting smaller and smaller. **No graphic detail.** Embers rise up past the camera.
- **SFX:** Joyful sound **cut to silence**, a long falling whoosh, a deep fire roar fading out.

## 4. CALL TO ACTION (CTA) ANIMATION (0:58 – 1:02)

- **VO:** *"Your next life… is decided by this one. Which path is yours? Comment… and share."*
- **Visual:** The screen **splits into two paths**: up toward the golden gate (Jannah) and down into the red glow. In the middle is a **glowing hourglass** with sand still falling (*you still have time*). The golden thread from Parts 1–2 comes into the middle and **points up**.
- **Text:**
  - Top: **"Part 1 · Part 2 · Part 3"** chips
  - Centre: **"YOU DECIDE YOUR NEXT LIFE."**
  - Bottom: **"Which path is yours? Comment 👇"** · **"Share = sadaqah jariyah 🤍"**
- **Loop:** Fade to the dark graveyard from the hook, so it replays seamlessly.
- **SFX:** A steady heartbeat, the ticking of the hourglass, a final warm-but-serious chord.

## 5. PACING & VIBE — BGM MAP

| Section | Mood | Music style |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Shock | Silence → **massive horn blast** (sound design) → rising wind |
| Mahsyar plain | Anxious, oppressive | Low drones, heat sizzle, slow heartbeat, no melody |
| Books | Suspense | Rising strings-like pad, flutter rush, sudden silence |
| Right vs. left | Contrast | Bright chime / dark low hit |
| Mizan | Max suspense | Ticking pulse, heartbeat stop, resolve to major (good) / minor brass (evil) |
| Hawdh | Relief | Cool, airy pads, water, chimes |
| Sirat | Fear + adrenaline | Fast pulse percussion, fire bed, wind, rising tension per speed |
| Jannah | Joy, majesty | Big warm swell, choir pads (voice only), birds, river |
| The fall | Dread | Hard cut to silence → fall whoosh → fire |
| CTA | Serious hope | Heartbeat + soft piano chord |

**Music-free version:** use only vocal pads and sound design. **Mix:** VO on top, BGM −12 dB under VO, export at −14 LUFS.
