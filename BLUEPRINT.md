# "WHO WILL ANSWER FOR YOU?" — 2D Islamic Animation Blueprint

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 24 fps · **Runtime:** 58 s · **Language:** English VO + English subtitles
**Voice:** One male narrator. Deep, calm, close-mic, slightly breathy. Starts conversational ("bro, look at this"), gets heavier, ends warm and hopeful. Think late-night storyteller, not a lecture.
**Audience:** Teens to grandparents. Phone-native visuals for youth, clear story and sincere message for adults.

---

## 0. RULES FOR RESPECTFUL CONTENT (read before animating)

| Topic | How to handle it |
|---|---|
| Angels Munkar & Nakir | **No faces, no human features.** Show them as huge forms made of deep blue and black light: glowing eyes like lightning, a voice that shakes the soil. The hadith describes them as *aswadani azraqani*, "black and blue". Keep them abstract and full of awe, never like cartoon monsters. |
| Women / "distracted by women" | **No sexualised drawings.** Show it as notifications, heart emojis and a dating-app swipe UI on the phone. The point is that his heart was busy, not the women. |
| The wife's aurah | Show her only as a **soft silhouette with a glowing outline**. Never draw her body. The message is about his responsibility, not about shaming her. |
| The deceased | We never say who goes to hell. Frame it as a warning: *"What if this is you?"* The ending gives hope (taubah, sadaqah). |
| Sources to show in the caption/description | The three questions & "Hah, hah, I don't know" + iron hammer: *Sunan Abu Dawud 4753* (hadith of al-Bara' ibn 'Azib). Deeds that continue after death: *Sahih Muslim 1631*. "Each of you is a shepherd…": *Bukhari 893*. Have an ustaz check the script before posting. |

## 1. VISUAL STYLE & AESTHETIC

**Style name: "Paper-Light Noir"**, flat 2D with a cut-paper look.

- **Characters:** A simple faceless main character (no eyes or mouth, just a head shape with a slight tilt). Kids and adults can see themselves in him, and it keeps things respectful. Thick rounded outlines, a light paper-grain texture, and 2–3 shades per colour.
- **Camera:** A fake 2.5D camera. Layered parallax (3–5 layers), slow push-ins, and a few fast "whip" moves timed to the beat. Everything is designed for the centre 4:5 safe zone so the UI on TikTok, Reels and Shorts doesn't cover it.
- **Motion language:**
  - *Dunya scenes*: snappy, bouncy squash-and-stretch, overshoot, bright pops. It feels like a fun app.
  - *Death and onward*: motion slows to about 50%, no bounce, heavy easing, floating dust. The fun is gone.
  - *Grave*: almost still, with only breathing light, falling soil grains and camera shake on impacts.
  - *Ending*: soft, warm and floating, with light particles rising.
- **Signature device:** A **thin golden thread** comes out of the man's chest at the start. It is his *amal* (deeds) connection. Every time he chooses dunya over duty, the thread frays. In the grave it **snaps**. At the end the viewer's own thread lights up again. This one visual metaphor carries the whole story.

### Colour palette (three acts)

| Act | Colours | Hex | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dunya (illusion)** | Neon magenta, electric cyan, gold glitter, on deep navy | `#FF2E88` `#22D3EE` `#F5C542` `#0B1026` | Exciting, loud, shallow |
| **Death & Barzakh** | Ash grey, bone white, bruise blue, ink black, and one red accent | `#6B7280` `#E8E4D9` `#1E3A8A` `#05060A` `#B91C1C` | Cold, empty, fear |
| **Hope (ending)** | Dawn peach, soft teal, warm gold, ivory | `#F7B79B` `#5EC4B6` `#E9B949` `#FFF8EC` | Peace, rahmah, calm |

**Typeface:** Captions in *Montserrat ExtraBold* (white, black 6 px stroke, highlight word in gold). Arabic phrases in *Amiri* or *Scheherazade New*. Max 4–5 words per caption line.

## 2. THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:03)

**Goal:** Stop the thumb with a pattern interrupt that looks like the viewer's own screen.

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8 s | **Full-screen fake phone feed.** Looks like the viewer's real app: a funny cat clip, a car video, likes flying. A cartoon thumb scrolls it fast. | — | Real *scroll-flick* SFX ×3, tiny pop sounds, a trendy beat (bright synth). |
| 0.8–1.4 s | The feed **freezes on a black post.** The beat cuts **instantly** (hard silence). A white heart-rate line runs across it… then goes **flat**. | **"YOU HAVE 3 SECONDS."** slams in word by word (scale 140%→100%, each word hits with a screen shake) | Heart monitor *beep… beep… beeeeeeep* (flatline). |
| 1.4–2.4 s | The text **cracks like glass.** Through the cracks, a dark grave and a faint blue light below. | Changes to: **"…to answer 3 questions."** (glitch/typewriter) | Glass crack. Deep sub-bass *BOOM* (40 Hz) that rumbles the phone. VO (whisper, close): *"What if your phone died… and you did too?"* |
| 2.4–3.0 s | **Match-cut transition:** camera pulls back out of the cracked screen and we see the screen is **the man's phone in his hand.** He keeps scrolling, not noticing. Colours flood back to neon. | Small caption top: **"Watch till the end. It's about YOU."** | A reverse-cymbal *whoosh* sucks us back in; the trendy beat restarts, slightly muffled. |

> Why it works: kids recognise the feed straight away, the flatline is a universal fear signal, and "3 questions" creates a curiosity gap that only the story pays off. The match-cut means there's no "intro". The hook *becomes* scene 1.

## 3. SCENE-BY-SCENE ANIMATION BLUEPRINT

> VO timing is about 2.6 words per second. Bracketed text in VO = delivery note.

### ACT 1 — THE DUNYA (0:03 – 0:18) · *Fun, flashy, slightly hollow*

---

**SCENE 1 — Scroll Addiction (0:03 – 0:06)**

- **VO:** *"Meet him. He had everything… except time for what mattered."*
- **Visual:** Over-the-shoulder at night. The phone light is the only light on his blank face. The room is messy. A **prayer mat rolled up in the corner gathers dust**, and the camera notices it with a tiny rack focus. The adhan clock on the wall shows **Isha** passing. Earbuds in, his head bobs. Music notes float out as small neon shapes that circle his head **like a cage**.
- **Text:** "He had everything…" fades in, then **"…except time."** slides up. The word *time* turns into a little clock that melts.
- **SFX:** Continuous scroll flicks, notification "ting-ting", muffled bass leaking from earbuds. A **distant adhan, very faint and muffled**, which he turns down (volume slider SFX *tick-tick-tick*).

---

**SCENE 2 — Heart Distracted (0:06 – 0:08)**

- **VO:** *"His heart was busy… chasing likes and DMs."*
- **Visual:** His phone screen fills the frame. Swipe-right UI, heart emojis rain down, DM bubbles stack up ("u up? 😏"). His **golden deed-thread** (introduced here: a thin glowing line from his chest going up) **frays one strand** with a tiny spark. Behind him, **his small son** stands holding a Qur'an *iqra* book, waiting. He waves him away without looking. The boy's shoulders drop.
- **Text:** **"Busy."** stamped in red over the son, like a "seen" status.
- **SFX:** Swipe *fwip*, heart pops, kiss-emoji "mwah" chirp (comic), then a tiny sad *thud* of the book closing. Child's small sigh.

---

**SCENE 3 — Chasing Rank (0:08 – 0:10)**

- **VO:** *"He chased the title. The promotion. The corner office."*
- **Visual:** A fast **vertical climb**. He runs up a staircase made of trophies and name plates ("Manager", "Director", "CEO"), stepping on other faceless coworkers. The camera whip-pans up with him.
- **Text:** Job titles **flip like a scoreboard** (split-flap animation) faster and faster.
- **SFX:** Fast footsteps on hard floor, split-flap clatter, office applause, a smug **"Hmph!"** proud breath. Corporate stinger.

---

**SCENE 4 — The Rich Life: everything DROPS IN (0:10 – 0:15)** *(signature "gedebuk" sequence, cut to the beat)*

- **VO:** *[proud, mocking tone]* *"Bugatti. Rolls-Royce. A villa on the hill. First class to everywhere…"*
- **Visual:** Each item **falls from the sky and slams down** with comic weight and a dust cloud. The ground cracks each time. He strikes a pose.
  1. **Hypercar** (generic sleek supercar silhouette, no logos) **SLAMS** down. Bounce. Headlights wink.
  2. **Luxury limousine** with a shining hood ornament shape (generic) **SLAMS** down next to it.
  3. **Giant villa** with pools and palm trees **SLAMS** down behind him, shaking the screen.
  4. **Travel montage:** a suitcase spins and is covered in stickers, Paris → Dubai → Bali → Tokyo, with passport stamps hitting the screen *thunk thunk thunk*.
  - With each landing, his golden thread **frays more**.
- **Text:** Each item's name **drops in with it** and bounces: **"BUGATTI." "ROLLS ROYCE." "VILLA." "✈ EVERYWHERE."**, plus a "+$" money counter ticking up in the corner.
- **SFX:** Whistle-fall → **heavy GEDEBUK impact** (deep thud + metal crunch + dust puff) ×3, a V16 engine **roar**, a rich-man **"Hahaha!"** laugh, coins cascading, cash-register *ka-ching*, camera shutter clicks (selfies), plane takeoff whoosh, passport stamp thunks.
- **Brand note:** Never use real logos or exact car designs. Say the names in VO/text only if your platform and legal check allow it; otherwise use "hypercar" and "luxury car".

---

**SCENE 5 — But Not Hajj (0:15 – 0:18)**

- **VO:** *[pause, softer]* *"He went everywhere… except the one place Allah invited him."*
- **Visual:** **Hard stop.** The music drops out. A spinning globe slows and **every country lights up** with pins, except **Makkah, which stays dark**. Push in on the empty pin. A dusty **Hajj registration letter** sits unopened on his villa table, stamped with older and older years (2019… 2022… 2025…). An **unpaid zakat notice** slides under the door. His **wife** (glowing silhouette only) waits at the dinner table alone. He is on his phone.
- **Text:** **"Everywhere…"** fades, then the second line **"…except Makkah."** appears in soft white, with a single slow pulse.
- **SFX:** Record-scratch stop, then near silence. A lone clock **tick… tick…**. Paper rustle. A soft, sad single piano note.

---

### ACT 2 — THE END (0:18 – 0:30) · *Shock, then cold silence*

---

**SCENE 6 — The Accident (0:18 – 0:20)**

- **VO:** *"Then one ordinary night…"* **[cut off by the crash]**
- **Visual:** Back in the hypercar, at night, rain. He's **driving while texting**. POV through the windshield: a truck's headlights flare **white**. **Smash cut to full white frame**, then black. (No gore. Only light, broken glass shards floating in slow motion, the phone spinning in the air with its screen cracking.)
- **Text:** None. Only the phone screen, mid-air, shows the last message: **"brb"**.
- **SFX:** Rain on glass, wipers, a phone notification ding, a **truck horn swelling**, tyre screech, **CRASH** (heavy layered impact), glass tinkling in slow motion, then a **high-pitched tinnitus ring** fading into total silence.

---

**SCENE 7 — The Soul Sees Itself (0:20 – 0:24)**

- **VO:** *[whisper]* *"His soul rose… and saw his own body. Still. Cold. No more chances."*
- **Visual:** Colours drain to **grey**. A translucent version of him (white, flickering) **sits up out of his body** at the roadside. He looks down at his own body lying still under a sheet in the ambulance light (red light pulse is the only colour). He reaches to touch his body, and **his hand passes through**. He grabs his phone and it shows **"No Signal"**. He falls to his knees and **screams**, but **no sound comes out**, just distorted air ripples.
- **Text:** **"No more chances."** Each word fades in slowly and stays heavy and still. Underneath, small Arabic: **إِنَّا لِلّٰهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ** fades in gently.
- **SFX:** Ethereal reverse wind, a slow heartbeat that **stops**, distant siren muffled like underwater, a **gasp of fear**, then a **muted scream** (real scream processed with a low-pass filter and reverb so it sounds trapped). His panicked breathing: *"no, no, no…"* whispered.

---

**SCENE 8 — Prepared, Washed, Carried, Buried (0:24 – 0:30)**

*Fast but gentle 4-shot montage. Each shot ~1.5 s, linked by the camera moving downward.*

- **VO:** *"They washed him. Wrapped him in white. Carried him… and left him alone in the ground."*
- **Visual:**
  1. **Ghusl:** Top-down view, kind hands (sleeves only) pour water from a jug. Water drops glow. His soul watches from the corner, shaking.
  2. **Kafan:** White cloth folds over in neat layers like paper origami. It is the only white in a grey world. *(Small detail: none of his riches are inside. Only cloth.)*
  3. **Janazah walk:** Silhouette crowd carries the keranda through a misty graveyard. Footsteps. The villa and cars appear in the far background, **getting smaller and fading**. Coworkers check their phones.
  4. **Burial:** Camera inside the grave looking **up** at a rectangle of sky. Soil drops in shovel by shovel and the light gets smaller and smaller, until it is a thin line… then **black**.
- **Text:** Four words, one per shot, centred, fading in and out: **"Washed." → "Wrapped." → "Carried." → "Alone."** The last one *Alone* stays for 1 second and shrinks into the dark.
- **SFX:**
  1. **Water pouring**, drips, a cloth wringing.
  2. **Cloth rustle**, soft fabric folding.
  3. **Many slow footsteps** on gravel, wind, a soft group murmur *"La ilaha illallah…"* fading.
  4. **Shovel scrape → soil thud on the coffin** ×4, each one louder and more muffled. Last thud = total black, total silence.

---

### ACT 3 — THE GRAVE (0:30 – 0:52) · *Fear, awe, regret*

---

**SCENE 9 — They Arrive (0:30 – 0:33)**

- **VO:** *[very low, slow]* *"Then the ground shook. Two angels came… Munkar and Nakir."*
- **Visual:** Pitch black. The grave walls (soil with roots) are lit faintly from below. **The soil walls crack and push in.** Two gigantic forms **rise behind the man**. They are made of swirling **deep blue and black smoke-light**, so tall they fill the whole 9:16 frame and go off the top. Only two pairs of **lightning-white eyes** open. No faces. Blue electric veins pulse through them. The man (small, bottom of frame) curls up in his kafan.
- **Text:** **"MUNKAR & NAKIR"** in thin, wide-spaced letters, rising slowly from the bottom with a blue glow, then dissolving into dust.
- **SFX:** **Earth-rumble** (sub-bass), soil cracking and crumbling, a **deep thunder roll**, low choir hum (no instruments), the man's **shaky breathing** close-mic.

---

**SCENE 10 — The Three Questions (0:33 – 0:40)**

- **VO:** *(Angels' voices = narrator's voice pitched down and doubled, with heavy reverb. Arabic first, then English in a whisper under it.)*
  - Angels: **"Man rabbuka?"** — Narrator: *"Who is your Lord?"*
  - Angels: **"Ma dinuka?"** — *"What is your religion?"*
  - Angels: **"Man nabiyyuka?"** — *"Who is your prophet?"*
  - Man: *[trembling, stuttering]* **"Hah… hah… I… I don't know…"**
- **Visual:** Each question is a **shockwave of blue light** that pushes the man back against the wall. When he tries to answer, **flashbacks flicker in his head like broken phone videos**: the car, the villa, the swipe screen, the scoreboard titles. All **glitch out and turn to static.** He searches his memory and finds nothing. His mouth opens; only **"…"** comes out of a speech bubble that crumbles.
- **Text:** Each Arabic question **slams in big and centred in Amiri font** with a screen shake and blue glow, with the English translation sliding underneath:
  - **مَنْ رَبُّكَ؟** — *Who is your Lord?*
  - **مَا دِينُكَ؟** — *What is your religion?*
  - **مَنْ نَبِيُّكَ؟** — *Who is your prophet?*
  - Then his answer appears in **shaky, jittering, small text**: **"Hah… hah… I don't know…"** and the letters fall apart.
- **SFX:** Each question = **deep BOOM + reverse reverb tail + thunder crack**. His answer = small voice, teeth chattering, a **whimper**, TV-static glitch sounds over the memory flashes.

---

**SCENE 11 — The Iron Hammer & The Silence Above (0:40 – 0:44)**

- **VO:** *"He was struck with a hammer of iron. He screamed… and no one above could hear him."*
- **Visual:** One of the angels raises a **giant iron hammer** made of dark metal and blue fire. **The swing is shown, never the impact.** At the moment of the hit: **cut to the surface**, a quiet sunny graveyard. Birds. People walking past, laughing, on their phones. **Total peace above.** Then **cut back below**: the grave walls **close in** tighter (squeeze animation), the soil presses on his ribs. His golden thread, already frayed, **SNAPS** in slow motion and the end floats away and dies out like a spark.
- **Text:** Above ground: **"Silence."** (tiny, calm, white). Below: **"No one hears."** shakes and fades. When the thread snaps: **"Disconnected."** appears like a phone error message.
- **SFX:** Iron swing **whoom**, then the **audio cuts to dead silence on impact** (more frightening than a loud hit) → peaceful birdsong and laughter above → back below: **crushing soil creak, bones/pressure groan (subtle)**, a muffled scream, and the thread snap = a single **violin string twang**.

---

**SCENE 12 — No Help Is Coming (0:44 – 0:50)**

- **VO:** *"No prayers came from his children. He never taught them. And his sins kept growing… because he never guided his family."*
- **Visual:** **Split screen, top and bottom.**
  - **Top (the world):** His grown son is on his phone, scrolling, the same way his father did. **No du'a.** Above the son's head, a "Sending du'a…" loading bar sits at **0%** and then shows **"Failed."**
  - **Bottom (the grave):** The man looks up and waits for light that never comes. Small **dark ink drops** keep falling onto him from above, adding weight. These are the continuing sins: his wife's silhouette (glowing outline only) walks out uncovered, and an ink drop falls on **him**, not her. A small caption on the drop reads *"his responsibility."*
  - A **counter** in the corner: **"Deeds received: 0"** stays at 0. **"Sins added: +1 … +2 … +7"** keeps climbing.
- **Text:** **"Deeds received: 0"** in cold grey, and a red ticking counter. Final line pulses: **"You are their shepherd."** *(Bukhari)*
- **SFX:** An empty "dial tone" or call-failed beep for the du'a loading bar, scrolling flicks from the son (echoing the opening, a sound callback), **ink drip "plip"** sounds, heavy sigh/groan of regret from the man: *"If only…"* (Surah al-Fajr 89:24 *"Ya laitani qaddamtu li hayati"* can be whispered softly).

---

**SCENE 13 — Busy in Barzakh (0:50 – 0:52)**

- **VO:** *"Now… his life in Barzakh is very busy."*
- **Visual:** Slow **pull-back** from the grave. It becomes a tiny dark rectangle among hundreds of graves, then the camera rises through clouds of grey. Text overlays a sand-timer whose sand **doesn't fall** (time frozen until Qiyamah).
- **Text:** **"BUSY."** in grey. It echoes Scene 2 where he stamped "Busy" on his son. **Same font, same red stamp, now on his own grave.**
- **SFX:** Wind, a deep low drone, the sand-timer creak. The red stamp *thunk* repeats from Scene 2 (sound callback, the viewer will feel it).

---

### ACT 4 — HOPE (0:52 – 0:58) · *Warm, hopeful, call to action*

---

**SCENE 14 — But You Are Still Here (0:52 – 0:56)**

- **VO:** *[voice changes: warm, close, hopeful]* *"But you… are still breathing. You can change your story, starting now. Do your duty. Teach your family. Give sadaqah. In your rizq is someone else's rizq."*
- **Visual:** **Colour floods back, but not neon. Dawn.** We cut to a **new man** (or the same character shape) waking at Fajr. Fast, warm, satisfying beats, ~0.8 s each:
  1. He **puts the phone face-down** and unrolls the dusty prayer mat (dust blows off in golden sparkles).
  2. He sits with his **son** and opens the iqra book together. The son smiles (a simple curved line).
  3. He gives his wife a **beautiful scarf as a gift**, shown as a silhouette with a warm glow. Respectful.
  4. He **hands a food pack to a poor old man**, and coins drop into a masjid box. From each act a **golden thread** rises up and connects to the sky.
  5. Plane ticket: **"Destination: Makkah."** He smiles.
  - The threads multiply into **a glowing web of light**. That is *sadaqah jariyah*, beneficial knowledge, and a righteous child: the three things that continue *(Muslim 1631)*.
- **Text:** Kinetic list, each word **pops in with a soft glow and a little bounce**:
  **"Pray."** → **"Teach."** → **"Guide."** → **"Give."** → **"Go."**
  Then a fade-in quote: *"In your rizq is someone else's rizq."* (handwritten-style, warm gold)
- **SFX:** Soft wind chimes, birdsong, a **gentle adhan of Fajr** (clear this time, not muffled, as a callback to Scene 1), pages turning, a child's soft giggle, coins clinking gently, a warm *shimmer* each time a golden thread appears.

## 4. CALL TO ACTION (CTA) ANIMATION (0:56 – 0:58 + loop)

- **VO:** *"Your phone will die one day. So will you. Share this… before it's too late. Who will answer for you?"*
- **Visual:** The golden threads from the last scene **join into a phone outline**, and the screen shows a **battery icon at 3%**, a callback to the hook's "3 seconds". The battery **pulses red**. A gentle finger taps the **"share" arrow**, which turns into a **flying paper plane of light** that splits into hundreds of tiny lights flying off-screen. These are the deeds passed on to others.
- **On-screen text (final card, 2 s):**
  - Top: **"Your battery is running out."** (small, white)
  - Centre (big, gold, slow pulse): **"WHAT WILL YOU ANSWER?"**
  - Bottom: **"Comment: Allah · Islam · Muhammad ﷺ"** with three small glowing buttons. This invites people to "answer the 3 questions" in the comments, which drives engagement and is meaningful.
  - Tiny: **"Share = sadaqah jariyah 🤍"**
- **Seamless loop:** The final frame **fades to the same black + flatline** used at 0:01. When the video loops, the hook replays naturally, which raises rewatch rate.
- **SFX:** Heart-monitor beep comes back but **steady and alive** (beep… beep…), a soft paper-plane whoosh, a final warm piano chord, silence.

## 5. PACING & VIBE: BACKGROUND MUSIC MAP

| Time | Section | Music style | BPM / key | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:01 | Hook feed | Trendy bright synth / phonk-lite loop | 140, major | Cuts to **silence** at 0:00.8 exactly |
| 0:01–0:03 | Flatline | **No music.** Sub-bass hit + heartbeat | — | Silence is the scare |
| 0:03–0:15 | Dunya | **Bouncy trap / lo-fi flex beat**, fat bass, finger snaps, slightly "too much" | 140 (half-time feel) | Slight muffle (low-pass at 6 kHz) so it feels hollow. Each GEDEBUK lands on beat 1 |
| 0:15–0:18 | Hajj missed | Beat drops out → **single music-box / piano note** with long reverb | — | First moment of reflection |
| 0:18–0:24 | Crash & soul | **No music**, only SFX and tinnitus → then a **dark ambient drone** (low strings, reversed pads) | ~60, D minor | Suspicious and cold |
| 0:24–0:30 | Janazah | **Sad cello / low humming choir** (vocals only, no instruments, for those who avoid music) | 60 | Slow, mourning |
| 0:30–0:44 | Grave | **Horror sound design**: sub-bass pulses, deep thunder, braams, dissonant cluster drone, heartbeat | Free time | The scariest part, and loudest. Use *silence on the hammer hit* |
| 0:44–0:52 | Regret | **Sad solo piano / nasheed-style hum**, minor key, sparse | 70, A minor | Emotional peak; let the VO breathe |
| 0:52–0:58 | Hope + CTA | **Warm uplifting nasheed-style vocal pad**, soft strings, rising swells, birdsong | 90, C major | Resolve to major. End on one warm chord |

**Music-free version (recommended to also export):** Many Muslim viewers avoid instruments. Make a version that uses **only vocal humming, nasheed-style pads (voice-only), and sound design**. It works almost as well, because most of the emotion here comes from SFX and silence.

**Mix levels:** VO always on top (−6 dB LUFS short-term peaks), BGM ducked −12 dB under VO, impacts allowed to peak. Export loudness: **−14 LUFS** integrated (TikTok/Reels/Shorts standard).

## 7. SUBTITLE STYLE

- Centre-lower third (above platform UI), max **2 lines, 4–5 words each**.
- **Word-by-word highlight** (karaoke style): the active word turns gold `#F5C542`.
- Impact words (BUGATTI, CRASH, ALONE, I DON'T KNOW) scale up 120% with a quick shake.
- In Act 3, subtitles turn **bone-white with a blue glow** and move slower, with no bounce.
- Arabic always paired with its English translation underneath.
